# Comic artwork

The site uses illustrated cutouts on the original 3D storefront. The artwork in `dist/assets/comic/` includes two six-pose animation strips, the characters at rest, small trail sprites, four editorial illustrations, and the separate layers for the pancake toss. The frame crops keep the generated alpha.

The original comic panels supplied the character designs, line weight and color reference. The assets were generated with the built-in image generator and packed by `scripts/pack-comic-assets.py`.

## lemrix

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for the website’s 3D shop scene. Create exactly six separate full-body drawings of the SAME mascot, in a strict 3-column, 2-row regular grid on a genuinely transparent canvas. Equal cells, generous empty padding around every silhouette, no overlap, no labels, no ground, no shadows outside the silhouette. Match size and proportions across cells. Top-left: idle at three-quarter front angle looking at viewer, both feet down. Top-middle: running to the right, left leg reaching forward, right arm forward. Top-right: running right at the passing pose with feet under body, arms passing. Bottom-left: running right in a small airborne stride, right leg forward, left arm forward. Bottom-middle: running right in the alternate passing pose, tucked feet, opposite arms. Bottom-right: happy greeting facing three-quarter front, one short paw lifted. Keep every tail, ear, bone and foot fully inside its own cell. Wide sheet with 3:2 proportions. Treat the references as exact character design and style references only.
Subject: Lemrix is exactly the blue mascot from the references, a smooth aqua tadpole/dinosaur-like blob with a big round slightly drooping head that flows into a soft pear body, tiny navy oval eyes, little mouth, thick stubby three-finger arms and feet, and one thick tapered curved tail. Bare blue skin. No ears, fins, horns, gills, clothes, or belly patch.
Match the attached Cook’d × Lemrix comic panels with very high fidelity. Hand-drawn two-dimensional indie comic, confidently uneven thick midnight-navy ink contours, slightly imperfect curves, flat aqua and warm biscuit fills, one darker flat shadow shape per form, very subtle dry pencil and halftone texture inside the colors. Small horizontal navy oval eyes and the expressive, slightly deadpan faces in the reference. Do not give the art any 3D shading, glossy highlights, gradients, realistic lighting, anime sparkle eyes, outer white sticker rim, or airbrush finish.
